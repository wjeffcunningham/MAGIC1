How the League Works

  • Premodern Constructed, proxies legal.
  • Three months (January–March), 4 assigned matches per month.
  • You’re placed into a colour pod (Emerald / Sapphire / Ruby / Pearl) with local players where possible.
  • League points come from these assigned matches; events at Stronghold / Connection are tracked separately as tiebreakers and Elo.
